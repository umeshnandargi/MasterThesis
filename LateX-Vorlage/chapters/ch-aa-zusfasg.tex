\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

This master's thesis investigates multi-objective optimization methods
for the design of robotic grippers used in bin-picking applications.
The aim is to compare selected optimization algorithms on two
gripper-design problems: the placement of three suction cups and the
design of rigid parallel-jaw contours. Both problems are treated as
black-box optimization tasks with multiple, potentially conflicting
objectives.

First, an overview of robotic grasping, gripper design, and
multi-objective optimization is presented. The principles of
evolutionary algorithms, Bayesian optimization, and performance
indicators are then introduced. A literature review examines existing
approaches to grasp planning, gripper design, and algorithm comparison.

Subsequently, the two gripper-design problems are formulated and
integrated into a common optimization framework. Suction-cup layouts
are assessed using grasp diversity, the number of retained grasps, and
the area spanned by the cups. Parallel-jaw contours are represented
by splines and evaluated using geometric proximity at four predefined
workpiece orientations.

NSGA-II, NSGA-III, MOEA/D, SMS-EMOA, and CMOPSO are compared on four
workpieces. Their results are assessed using inverted generational
distance plus, hypervolume, and runtime. For the suction-cup problem,
SMS-EMOA achieves the lowest mean inverted generational distance plus
on all four workpieces. For the parallel-jaw problem, NSGA-II achieves
the highest mean archive hypervolume on all four workpieces.

Bayesian optimization is also investigated. The main attempt is stopped
because of its runtime and produces no final result files. A subsequent
single-run test on the parallel-jaw gripper and connecting rod provides
indicative results but does not support a conclusive comparison.

Finally, the limitations of the study and directions for future work
are discussed. These include further Bayesian experiments, improved
reproducibility, non-overlap constraints for the suction cups, and
physical validation of the optimized gripper designs.

\vspace{1em}
\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} multi-objective optimization, robotic
gripper design, bin picking, suction-cup gripper, parallel-jaw gripper, evolutionary algorithms, Bayesian
optimization
